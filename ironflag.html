    Online
    Host a match and wait in the open lobby, or tap someone else's game to join. Both sides get a full base with turrets, gates, bridges and infantry, same as Skirmish, and the enemy bunker is across the water. Same controls as above. Your name shows in the lobby so friends can find your game
    How to win
    One enemy building hides their flag. Destroy buildings to expose it, then drive a jeep over it and back onto your bunker pad. Park on your bunker pad to swap vehicles, or stop beside a fuel depot or ammo tent to top up. Park next to a hospital (red cross on the roof) to repair, an ammo depot for a fast full rearm, or a gas station for a fast refuel — yours or the enemy's. With the jeep, Shift inflates its tires to float across deep water. Stop or creep the jeep next to your own soldiers and up to 3 hop in and shoot out of the windows, but every hit the jeep takes kills one of them. Stray out to sea and a submarine will find you. Base gates open only for their own side, so blast the enemy's open first. Explosives drop bridges, and soldiers can be run over. Point the tank turret at a drone and the shell aims up to hit it. Esc pauses, M music.
